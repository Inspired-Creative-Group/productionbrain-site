## The message (locked by Juan, 2026-11-10; do not rewrite)
- **Problem:** "Every AI you use knows a piece of your business. None of them share it. None of it is yours."
- **Answer:** "One brain for every AI and everyone on your team. You own it."
- **Test for any new line:** would it still be true if OpenAI shipped it tomorrow? Lean on the two things a vendor can't offer: it works across every AI tool, and the business owns it.
- **Retired 2026-11-09:** "your AI forgets", "re-briefing", "every chat starts from zero". Every major AI now remembers its user.
- A team is people plus AI agents. On the page say "AI" until the Proof section; "AI agents" is introduced there. "Memory" is the vendors' word; ours is "the brain".

## The offer (what the page sells today)
One brain for the business, on a dedicated machine (a Mac Studio) in the client's studio, built by hand and maintained remotely by ICG over a private encrypted network. It keeps its own notes, checks itself every night for stale or contradicting facts, and never deletes. Build fee plus monthly maintenance; hardware bought by the client at Apple's price; 90-day refund of the build fee on standard pricing.

**The page shows no prices** (since 2026-10-16). Prices are locked in the vault (`2026-10-04-production-brain-pricing-LOCKED`); what the page displays is Juan's call. Founding offer: one studio, free build, 30% off maintenance for the first year, no deadline.

## Audience (ICP)
AI-forward multimedia producers and small studios (video, design, audio): solo producers running a team of agents, and creative directors or studio leads where each person already uses a different AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Grok). Agencies that need to show a client who did what with AI. Objections heard: "my tools already have memory" (true, but per person and per vendor), "a folder solves memory" (keep the folder; add history, scoped keys, search by meaning, nightly cleanup), "I could build this myself" (the value is the upkeep), "what happens if it breaks".

## True today (checked 2026-11-16; re-check before quoting numbers)
- Claude Code, claude.ai, ChatGPT, Codex, Grok and Perplexity read and write the same brain.
- More than one computer and more than one person: the business partner runs Codex and Claude Code on her own Mac, against the same brain, with her own login.
- **Per-person attribution:** every save through the online gate records who was signed in, and the dashboard names them. Saves made directly on the main machine show where they came from, not a person.
- Handoffs between AI agents with no copy-paste, across two computers (proven 2026-11-15).
- Every agent signs its handoffs with its own key, and one handoff log is written only by the main machine, so no agent can rewrite it.
- A live board (Agent Deck) shows every agent and handoff; its demo view hides private text.
- Local models, nightly self-checks, where every fact came from, encrypted off-site backups, restore tested (full restore 2026-11-08, weekly read-back check).
- The business owns the machine and the files.

## Not true yet (never claim)
- Each person sees only what they're allowed to see. Anyone let in sees everything. A separate brain per project is what ICG builds when a client needs it; say it that way, never as running today.
- "Can't be faked", or that the production record itself is signed. Say "signed and logged" for handoffs.
- Scheduled pulls from Notion or Drive as standard (built at setup, on request).
- That a team can't share anything inside one vendor (shared projects partly do).
- Insurance or copyright requirements as a reason to buy. The 2026-11-14 research did not support it.
- "Every conversation recorded".
